## Important Data & Prototype Notice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Demonstration Data Disclaimer:** 
> The dataset in **`data/local_opportunities.csv`** contains simulated prototype/demonstration data representing local job vacancies, vocational training centers, and micro-enterprise clusters across sample districts (Indore, Jaipur, Udaipur, Bhopal, Lucknow, Patna, Delhi NCR).
>
> **This data is for prototype demonstration purposes only** and does not represent live production employment opportunities unless integrated with an official government API (such as NCS - National Career Service or Skill India Digital Hub).
